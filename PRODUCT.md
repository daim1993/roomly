# Roomly Product

## Register

product

## Users

Community members, server owners, moderators, guests, and platform administrators using Roomly to communicate in persistent text channels, join live voice and video rooms, share screens, manage communities, and operate a self-hosted instance.

## Product Purpose

Roomly provides a private, self-hosted communication workspace that keeps messaging, live calls, community structure, identity, and moderation in one browser-based product. Success means people can orient themselves quickly, move between conversations without friction, and trust the interface during both casual community use and operational admin work.

## Brand Personality

Intelligent, calm, and forward-looking. Roomly should feel precise and premium without becoming decorative, theatrical, or difficult to use.

## Anti-references

Avoid gaming-style neon spectacle, glassmorphism-heavy dashboards, skeuomorphic controls, ornamental gradients, heavy shadows, dense visual noise, ambiguous custom controls, and motion that delays the user's task.

## Design Principles

1. Typography establishes hierarchy before color or decoration.
2. Familiar communication patterns should disappear into the workflow.
3. Accent color communicates action, status, or meaningful data only.
4. Dense information remains calm through alignment, spacing, and repetition.
5. Motion explains state changes and never competes with conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, readable type at compact sizes, touch targets suitable for mobile use, and states that remain understandable without relying on color alone.
